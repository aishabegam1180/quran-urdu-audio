# Privacy Policy – Quran Safar – Urdu Tarjuma

**Effective date:** 21 November 2026

Quran Safar – Urdu Tarjuma ("the app") is a free, non-profit Android app made by Aisha, Bangalore, India, for listening to the Holy Quran with its Urdu translation. The app has no ads, no sign-up and no in-app purchases.

## Summary

**The app does not collect, store on any server, sell or share any personal information.** Everything you do in the app stays on your phone.

## Information kept only on your phone

To work, the app saves the following on your own device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else:

- where you stopped listening, so you can continue later
- your bookmarks, notes, favourite duas and settings (theme, playback speed, Islamic date adjustment, translation choice)
- surah audio files you choose to download for offline listening

All of this is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

## Location (Qibla only)

The Qibla screen uses your phone's location and compass to show the direction of the Kaaba. Location is asked for only when you open the Qibla screen, is used only on your phone to calculate the direction and distance, and is never stored, sent or shared. The rest of the app works with location turned off.

## Internet use

The app connects to the internet only to download the list of surahs and the Quran audio files, which are hosted on GitHub (github.com). As with any website, GitHub's servers receive basic technical information such as your IP address when a file is downloaded; the app sends nothing else. GitHub's own privacy statement applies to its servers: https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

## Feedback

If you choose to send feedback, the app opens your own email app with a message addressed to us. We receive only what you decide to send, such as your email address and message, and use it only to reply and improve the app.

## Advertising, analytics and tracking

The app contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking tools.

## Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
